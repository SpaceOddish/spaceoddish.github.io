# Ao3 wrapped: 2025
*My reading stats for the year 2025: 30 December 2024 - 1 January 2026*

Welcome to my yearly fanfic reading stats. I originally published stats for 2025 here on [bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/witnessmeme.bsky.social/post/3mbf5gv37ts2p). There might be tiny differences with what I posted there because I found some duplicated rows plus when talking about totals for all years of my record-keeping I am already including data for 2026.

## Data source
I bookmark all I read via the ao3 bookmark feature. Then, at the end of the year, I use this [bookmarklet](https://random.fangirling.net/scripts/ao3_works_stats/) to download all entries for the year into csv. I used to merge the csv files into a Google Sheet and calculate the averages and sums all whatnot through the formulas. But this was done in R with tidyverse packages.

## General stats
### Number of fics
In 2025, I read and bookmarked **179 fics** or **15 fics on average** per month. Compared to 2024, it was 146 fewer fics. When it comes to fandoms I read in 2025, it was just Destiel and Supernatural fandom.

### Words
When it comes to words, **I read 4,571,033 words** in 2025 (6,796,530 fewer words than in 2024). Kind of insane how much I read in 2024 but that's something that deserves another post.
![Jitter plot of all the fics I've read in 2025 by wordcount.](/plot_words_2025.svg)

### How many Hobbits?
If I wanted to compare this number of words to The Hobbit, it would be **48 Hobbits**. Compared to last year, 71 fewer Hobbits.

### Absolute faves: fics I really looooved
Out of all these fics I read in 2025, I tagged **absolute faves** as those I particularly loved. I tagged **28** fics as my absolute faves, **17 fewer** when compared to 2024.
